# Data access and regeneration

Historical raw PolyGraphs simulation outputs are not distributed. The supplement provides reconstructed canonical configurations and instructions for fresh regeneration.

The College Message topology is supplied and can also be reconstructed from the public SNAP source. The Francis Bacon dataset is excluded from the public checkout because redistribution terms for the exact snapshot have not been established. Its six Experiment 3 conditions require that snapshot to be obtained separately and placed at `supplement/networks/francisbacon-2023-04-23.gml`. See `supplement/NETWORK_SOURCES.md` for the snapshot description and `supplement/SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` for its checksum and provenance.

For questions about the historical simulation archive, contact the corresponding authors.
